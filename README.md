KEY MANAGEMENT INSIGHTS

01 — Growth
Customer growth is the primary driver of recurring revenue expansion.

02 — Profitability
EBITDA margin improves from 45.9% → 51.9%, while gross margin increases from 76% → 78%.

03 — Cash & Financing
Operating cash generation strengthens while scheduled debt repayment reduces debt from $2.0M → ~$1.66M.

04 — Watchpoint
ARPC declines to approximately $3,358 as the customer base expands, while NRR remains at 97%, making customer mix, acquisition efficiency and retention important areas to monitor.

THE MANAGEMENT STORY

Customer growth → Recurring revenue growth → Improving profitability → Stronger cash generation → Declining debt → Stronger debt-service capacity
